# Camera / Smile Frame surface

Scope: camera screen (`#camera`) and the saved 1080x1350 photo; mockups in `mockups/` first. Visitor mode: Persuade (the action is taking and sharing the photo).
Audience/job: Pune citizens on their own phone, mobile data, often outdoors in morning light; take a selfie in seconds and share it.
Constraints: frame must cover far less of the screen than the old cream card + 280px ring; preview must equal the saved photo; Sakal logo placeholder; English now, Devanagari later.
References chosen by user: BeReal / Instagram camera. Lead colour kept: amber/orange.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the face is the hero, the brand is the signature. Refuses the old poster card where branding ate the screen.
OWN-WORLD: camera-native black chrome, one amber (#F5A623) and orange (#F28C38) brand voice, warm near-black ink, white; Anek Latin (Ek Type, Devanagari sibling) in heavy and wide cuts; rounded 4:5 viewfinder; one amber shutter.
STORY: open, see yourself large, tap once, 3-2-1, get a branded photo identical to the viewfinder, share.
FIRST VIEWPORT: slim top bar (back, logo); full-width 4:5 viewfinder with the frame overlay drawn live; amber shutter centred below with one hint line.
FORM: canon (category standard: camera-first social campaign, BeReal/Instagram calibre); seed key 36f2ece3.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
