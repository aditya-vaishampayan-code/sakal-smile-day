Privacy notice

Pune Smile Day, Sakal Media Group · Last updated 13 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to your photo when you add it to the Pune Smile Day Smile Wall at bharatsmitdin.com. It is given under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your Smile Frame photo, only if you tap “Add to the Smile Wall” and give your consent. Your face, and the faces of anyone with you, are personal data.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or location.
  • Your internet address (IP address) is used for a moment to stop abuse, such as one network sending thousands of photos. It is not stored.

Taking a photo, downloading it or sharing it from your phone does not send it to us. Only “Add to the Smile Wall” does.

Why we use it

Only to show your photo publicly on the Smile Wall and to count the smiles for the Pune Smile Day campaign. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to identify you.

How long we keep it

Every photo is deleted by 3 November 2026. Uploads close at 11:30 PM on 2 November, and deletion starts at midnight. We keep no backups.

Photos you share yourself, for example on WhatsApp, Instagram or X, are under your control and are not covered by this deletion.

Your consent and your rights

  • Delete your photo at any time from the same phone: tap the yellow bin on your photo, or “Delete my photo” at the bottom of the Smile Wall. It is removed from the wall and deleted from our storage straight away.
  • If you added the photo from a phone you no longer have, email us (below) with the date and time you added it and a short description, and we will find and delete it.
  • You may also ask us about the data we hold, or raise a complaint, using the contact below. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Photos of anyone under 18 may only be added by their parent or guardian, who gives consent on their behalf.

Who handles your photo

Sakal Media Group runs the Smile Wall. The photos are stored for us by our technology provider, Cloudflare, only to run this campaign. Our moderators may remove any photo that is offensive or breaks the law.

Contact and grievances

Email ccare@esakal.com with the subject “Pune Smile Day photo”.
